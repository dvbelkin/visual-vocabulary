# Europe map source

`europe-110m.geo.json` is a reduced extract of Natural Earth
`ne_110m_admin_0_countries` containing European countries except Russia, plus
Turkey. Only the `name` property and geometry are retained.

- Source: <https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector>
- Scale: 1:110m
- License: public domain
- Terms: <https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/>
- Retrieved: 2026-07-26

Russia is omitted because its transcontinental geometry makes the compact
European teaching viewport unreadable. The examples are synthetic and do not
claim complete political or statistical coverage.
